How I work

I start with the decision, not the model.

The first question is what someone needs to do differently. For the airline network, that led to node-removal simulations that show which hubs are critical and where to reroute.

I distrust a good score.

In my credit-risk audit, 99.2% accuracy fell to a PR-AUC of 0.082 once I removed target leakage. Ablations, red-teaming and EU AI Act checks are how I make sure results hold up.

I leave things runnable.

Every project ends with documentation the next person can use: a handoff guide for the internship pipeline, a model card and risk register for the audit.
